# Asset sources

- `hero.png`: custom image generated from the owner's visual reference using the built-in image generation tool. Baki Hanma is a character from Baki; the artwork does not imply an official affiliation. Prompts in `../docs/ART-DIRECTION.md`.
- `channel-orange.jpg`: original Frank Ocean channel ORANGE artwork supplied by the owner, copied without alteration. Also embedded unchanged in the music SVGs. Album artwork and character rights remain with their respective owners; the repository software license does not grant rights to these third-party works.
- `icons/*.svg`: [Simple Icons 11.14.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.14.0), distributed under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.14.0/LICENSE.md). Brand marks remain subject to their respective owners' trademark rights. Download source: `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11.14.0/icons/SLUG.svg`.
- `brand/alx-official.svg`: the official white ALX logo from [ALX Africa's website](https://www.alxafrica.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/footer-logo.svg), embedded without redrawing. ALX retains its brand rights; this personal coursework presentation does not imply endorsement.
- `fonts/Poppins-*.ttf`: [Poppins from Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/poppins), licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The license is included in `fonts/OFL.txt`. Graphics embed glyph outlines produced with the pinned `opentype.js` build dependency.
- `icons/lucide/*.svg`: [Lucide 0.468.0](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/0.468.0), ISC license. Used for consistent interface, project, and activity icons.
- `icons/mysql-mark.svg` and `icons/linux-mark.svg`: [Devicon 2.16.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.16.0), MIT license. Brand marks retain their owners' rights. The generated cards render these marks in monochrome.
- `moonlit-coast.png`: custom scenery generated with the built-in image generation tool. `moonlit-coast.jpg` and `hero-texture.jpg` are JPEG encodings of the generated originals for lightweight embedding; dimensions and composition are preserved. Prompt in `../docs/ART-DIRECTION.md`.
- Layouts, SVG masks, grayscale calendar, and music controls are generated by the repository's scripts.
- `project-art/{e-esj,nticemploi,tracksnap,pictracker}.{png,jpg}`: four distinct editorial backgrounds created with the built-in image generation tool. The PNG originals are retained; the JPEGs are same-dimension encodings for SVG embedding. Full prompts and filenames are in `../docs/PROJECT-ART.md`.
- Public activity graphics are generated by `scripts/update-stats.mjs` from the sources recorded in `data/public-stats.json`.
- Local preview only: [github-markdown-css 5.8.1](https://github.com/sindresorhus/github-markdown-css), MIT. It is not a README dependency.
