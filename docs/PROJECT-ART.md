# Featured project backgrounds

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Original PNGs and same-dimension JPEG encodings (Sharp, quality 80) are saved in `assets/project-art/`. Each card embeds its own JPEG with a fading SVG mask. These are editorial illustrations, not screenshots of the applications.

## e-esj

Files: `assets/project-art/e-esj.png`, `assets/project-art/e-esj.jpg`.

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a finished wide 2.4:1 landscape background illustration for a premium grayscale developer portfolio project card. Monochrome black, charcoal and restrained silver, refined cinematic ink and photographic texture. Nearly pure black empty left half for readable overlaid text, subject concentrated in the right third, atmospheric soft falloff to the left. Elegant detail without clutter; no text, no lettering, no words, no numbers, no UI, no logos, no watermarks, no border. The result must feel like a subtle editorial background plate, not an app screenshot. Subject: a modern healthcare setting, sculptural stethoscope tubing and its silver circular chestpiece in the foreground with faint architectural glass of a hospital beyond. Cool moonlit highlights, calm and precise, no people or medical records.

## pictracker

Files: `assets/project-art/pictracker.png`, `assets/project-art/pictracker.jpg`.

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a finished wide 2.4:1 cinematic grayscale background illustration for PicTracker, a developer portfolio project about tracking profile picture changes for public social-media usernames. A refined dark digital archive: several luminous circular avatar portraits floating in a chronological sequence, connected by a thin delicate timeline, with softly layered earlier versions behind them. Use stylized anonymous monochrome human silhouettes inside the avatar circles, with clearly different portrait poses or hairstyles suggesting updates over time. No recognizable real people. Restrained black, charcoal and silver palette, fine glass reflections, subtle depth, premium editorial atmosphere. Keep the entire left half almost pure black and empty for text overlay, concentrate the avatar history imagery in the right half, fade gently into black toward the left. The image must communicate social profile picture history and digital identity, not photography equipment. No cameras, lenses, film, paper photos, clocks, text, lettering, numbers, logos, watermarks, borders, or busy app interface.
